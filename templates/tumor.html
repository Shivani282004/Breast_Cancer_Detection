Benign Tumors

Benign tumors are non-cancerous growths that typically do not spread to other parts of the body. While they are usually not dangerous, they may cause discomfort or pressure on surrounding tissues. Treatment may involve monitoring or surgical removal if necessary for symptom relief.

Malignant Tumors

Malignant tumors are cancerous and can spread to other areas of the body, damaging healthy tissue. They require prompt diagnosis and treatment, often involving surgery, chemotherapy, or radiation therapy. A biopsy is performed to assess the tumor's behavior and plan the appropriate course of action.
