About Breast Cancer

Breast cancer is a disease in which cells in the breast grow uncontrollably. It is one of the most common cancers worldwide, affecting both women and men, though predominantly women. Early detection and treatment significantly improve survival rates.

Risk Factors

While breast cancer can affect anyone, certain factors increase the risk, including:

  • Being female and over 50 years of age
  • A family history of breast or ovarian cancer
  • Genetic mutations like BRCA1 or BRCA2
  • Prolonged exposure to estrogen through early menstruation or late menopause
  • Lifestyle factors such as alcohol consumption and obesity

Key Symptoms

Early symptoms of breast cancer may include:

  • An unusual lump in the breast or underarm
  • Changes in breast size, shape, or texture
  • Persistent pain in the breast or nipple area
  • Nipple discharge that is not breast milk
  • Skin changes, such as dimpling or redness
